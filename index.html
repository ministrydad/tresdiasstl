Who We Are

Tres Dias of St. Louis

We are an organization dedicated to strengthening and extending the Body of Christ by bringing Christians to a closer, more personal walk with the Lord Jesus Christ and encourage them to Christian leadership and Apostolic Action in their environments.

Tres Dias of St. Louis is a lay-led movement with active participation of Clergy. We are a non-profit organization and actively seek the participation of people from all the Christian denominations in our area.

Connect

Find Christian friendship and community that lasts long after the weekend ends.

Serve

Grow
